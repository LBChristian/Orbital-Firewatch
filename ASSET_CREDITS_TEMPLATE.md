## Data labels
- LIVE: Retrieved successfully during this session. Still show original satellite acquisition timestamp.
- CACHED: Previously retrieved NASA data reused because of cache or upstream unavailability.
- HISTORICAL: User-selected or scenario-selected archive data.
- SYNTHETIC DEMO: Invented test/tutorial data, not NASA observations.
